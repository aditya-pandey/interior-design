How it is measured

How the quantities are measured (all from house_r1.json):

- Floors: room polygons (net). Option A Italian marble +15% wastage; Option B 1600 x 800 vitrified +8%, 2 tiles per box. Skirting = walled perimeter minus doors.

- Tiles: washroom walls full height to the 2.6 m false ceiling, minus doors and windows, +10%. Waterproofing = floor + 300 mm up the walls (more in the shower: 1.8 m).

- Paint: interior walls to the false ceiling minus openings, tiles, veneer cladding, fluted walls and the kitchen backsplash; ceilings = POP area; exterior = non-party external walls x storey height, parapets and boundary walls (both faces where both are exposed). Coverage rates are indicative.

- POP: ceilings of type POP (gypsum board 12.5 mm on GI framing, POP-finished), boards +10%; cove length = room perimeter where the ceiling has a cove.

- Granite: counter tops (20 mm black granite, moulded front), sills on every window (wall + 50 mm), thresholds on every door, stair treads / risers / landings (or marble to match the lobby), flamed granite paving. +10% wastage.

- Timber: door frames 125 x 60 mm (teak for the main door), shutters 35 mm flush with veneer (main door 40 mm teak); pergola hardwood; handrail; walnut veneer cladding (lobby, foyer, guest lounge, per the PDF schedule) and fluted walnut feature walls.

- MDF: each wardrobe / storage unit = carcass (sides, top, bottom, a vertical every 900 mm, 4 shelves if tall) + shutters (18 mm) + back (8 mm); +15% on 8 x 4 ft sheets. BWP ply for the vanities, the utility counter, the kitchen sink base and units on the terrace (owner: MDF elsewhere).

- Lights: counts from the electrical points; cove LED = cove length; under-cabinet LED = the cabinet runs.

- Pipes: lengths along the modelled routes and stacks, 3 m lengths +10%; bends counted where a route changes direction (tees and couplers extra).

- Wires: route length of each circuit x 3 cores (phase, neutral, earth) +10%; 90 m coils; sub-main sizes indicative (electrician to size).

Not included: labour, adhesives other than noted, hardware beyond hinges, sanitaryware, glass, aluminium windows, the lift, ACs and appliances.
