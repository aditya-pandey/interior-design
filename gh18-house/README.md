# GH-18 house model

To-scale model of GH-18, Celebrity Homes, Palam Vihar, Gurugram (owner: Deepti Jain; client on the architect's drawing: Mr. Praveen Kumar Yadav), built from the architect's GF/FF plan, plus a proposed second floor.

**New to the project (human or AI agent)? Start with `HANDOVER.md` (also `HANDOVER.pdf`).**

**`house.json` is the single source of truth.** The plans, the dimension check, and later the 3D viewer and the Blender renders are all generated from it.

## Status

## Layout

```
house.json          walls, openings, rooms, outdoor areas, stair, slabs, furniture (mm)
ASSUMPTIONS.md      every guess, deviation from the drawing and brief change
plans/              GF.svg, FF.svg, SF.svg + check_report.txt (generated)
inputs/             architect's PDF, 300 dpi raster, reference PNGs, pdf_dims.json
tools/              houselib.py (geometry), plans.py, check_dims.py, pdf_extract.py
viewer/ render/ renders/ assets/   phases 2 and 3
```

## Regenerate

Python 3.9+, standard library only:

```bash
python3 tools/check_dims.py   # house.json vs every PDF dimension string -> plans/check_report.txt
python3 tools/plans.py        # plans/GF.svg, FF.svg, SF.svg
```

`tools/pdf_extract.py` re-reads the dimension strings from the PDF into `inputs/pdf_dims.json`. It needs `pip install pdfplumber`, and only has to run if the PDF changes.

## 3D viewer (Phase 2)

Needs Node 18+.

```bash
cd gh18-house/viewer
npm install
npm run dev          # opens http://localhost:5173
```

The viewer reads `../house.json` directly, so Vite reloads it when the file is saved. Use `npm run build && npm run preview` for a production build. Append `?low` to the URL to switch off ambient occlusion and use a smaller shadow map on a weak GPU.

**What it does:**
- **Dollhouse orbit:**
  - drag to orbit, right-drag to pan, scroll to zoom
  - floor buttons GF / FF / SF / All (keys 1, 2, 3, 0)
  - an explode slider that separates the floors
- **Cutaway** (C): walls, frames and glass above 1.2 m turn transparent.
- **Walk** (first person):
  - WASD or arrow keys to move, Shift to run, mouse to look; Esc releases the mouse and Q leaves walk mode
  - eye height 1.6 m
  - collides with walls, glass and furniture
  - follows floors, steps, terraces and the stairs between storeys
- **View dropdown:** overview cameras, the front and rear exteriors, and one camera per room or outdoor area.
- **Day / Dusk** (N):
  - a physical sky with the sun placed for an east-facing entrance (late-afternoon sun from the south-west)
  - at dusk, warm cove strips, downlights, room lights, the dining pendant and the mandir glow come on
- **Room names and sizes** (L): labels showing each room's name, size and area.
- **Rendering:** ACES tone mapping, soft sun shadows, ambient occlusion (GTAO) and 4× MSAA.
- **Phones:**
  - the panel is a bottom bar with a Menu button that opens the controls as a sheet; picking a view closes it
  - overview cameras pull back to fit a portrait screen
  - in walk mode the left thumb is a joystick and the right thumb looks around

**Screenshots:** `npm run build && npm run shots` drives headless Chromium through every preset and mode, writing the images to `viewer/screenshots/`.

**Textures:** the procedural CC0 sets in `assets/textures/` are generated by `python3 tools/gen_textures.py`. `polyhaven.com` was blocked where this was built. On a machine with internet access, run `python3 tools/fetch_polyhaven.py` once: it downloads the Poly Haven CC0 sets and HDRIs and switches the viewer over to them. Licences are listed in `assets/LICENSES.md`.

**Car:** the parking bay holds the family's 2022 Maruti Suzuki Baleno in Grandeur Grey. It is `assets/models/baleno.glb`, by BHP3D under CC BY 4.0, recoloured and optimised. The credit is shown in the viewer panel and in `assets/LICENSES.md`.

**Paintings (`/withpainting`):** the family's own paintings hang only on the `/withpainting/` page of the hosted site, or locally with `?art`. The main page leaves them out.
- **Data:** they live in `house.json` under `floors[].art`. Furniture that differs between the two versions carries a `variant` (`art` / `no_art`).
- **Textures:** `tools/prep_art.py` builds them in `assets/art/` from the client's photos, which are not in the repo.

## Film

`renders/film/GH-18_film.mp4` is a 54 s cinematic film of the current model: 1920 × 1080 at 24 fps, with 2.39:1 letterbox bars and an original score. To rebuild it, run these from `gh18-house/viewer`:

```bash
npm run build
node film/render.mjs                                      # renders every shot's frames (resumable; about 5 s per frame headless)
python3 film/post.py ../renders/film/GH-18_film.mp4       # edit, grade, titles, score, encode
```

- **Shots:** `film/shots.mjs` holds the shot list: camera paths in model coordinates, cut on the bars of the 96 BPM score.
- **Score:** `film/score.py` synthesises the score.
- **Context:** the neighbourhood (flanking houses, street, trees, lamps) appears only in the film view (`?cinema`). It is scenery, not part of `house.json`.

## By the sea (`/play/`, work in progress on branch `play-v1`)

A free-roam page: GH-18 alone on an imagined coast. It opens with the home tour itself (exactly as on `/v1/`); walking up to the Baleno in walk mode offers "Get in the car", which opens up the rest. Drive the Baleno along a ~3 km coastal highway loop, get out and walk, and spend time in Deepti's Studio on the beach: two storeys and a roof terrace (a library with a round bookshelf reading nook, a lounge facing the sea with a window bed and a record player for her music, a gallery of her paintings, an oak stair hung with more of them, her atelier upstairs with easels, a wall of her poems (English and Hindi) and a mood board of her photos, floor seating and loungers on the terrace for the sunset, her music). Not linked from the tour, and `noindex`.

- **In the house (walk mode):** E to sit on a sofa, chair or bed (or lie down), E or W to get up.
- **Controls:** W / S drive, brake and reverse; A / D steer; Space handbrake (drifts); C camera (chase / driver's seat / bonnet / high); B look behind; F get out or in; E read, look, play music, sit or lie down; Shift walk faster; drag to look; P play / pause music, N next; T time of day; G go to the studio; R back home; L lights; H horn; M sound; Tab (or ☰) opens the menu with every control. In the house a chip by the clock names the room, and the minimap shows the floor plan. O plays the music in this room or everywhere; Go to… (in the menu) jumps to any room, the studio or the court. At the court's bench E starts a game of badminton to 11 (see below). In the house: the lift (E at its door), the mandir's diyas, chai on the hob. Phones get on-screen steering and pedals (on foot: Gas walks, arrows turn).
- **Code:** `viewer/src/play/`: `site.js` (layout), `beach.js` (land, sand, highway, palms, lamps, ground model), `water.js` (the sea: swells, reflections, clear shallows, foam), `night.js` (stars, Milky Way, moon, shooting stars), `post.js` (ambient occlusion, glow, tone mapping), `surfaces.js` and `textures.js` (material detail; the optional photo-scanned textures), `studio.js` (Deepti's Studio), `cosy.js` (curtains, candles, the beach steps, fire pit and hammock), `vehicle.js` (car physics), `carmodel.js` (the Baleno with split wheels, a turning steering wheel, lamps, suspension), `collide.js` (2D collisions), `audio.js` (synthesised engine, road, wind, tyres, surf), `music.js` (YouTube player for her songs), `main.js` (everything else).
- **Personal pack:** `viewer/play/deepti/` holds her paintings, poems, photos (the album), song list, birthday and the optional studio message (`studioMessage` in `pack.json`). Her own songs as audio files: put them in `viewer/play/deepti/music/` and list them in `pack.json` as `"tracks": [{ "file": "music/song.mp3", "title": "…" }]` (listed now: `music/nawazish.mp3`, Nawazish — Papon, and `music/kali-kali-zulfon-ke.webm`, Kali Kali Zulfon Ke — Madhur Sharma). The `music/` folder is gitignored: the songs are copied in by hand on each computer and never pushed (the repository is public); listed songs whose file isn't there are skipped, and with none the YouTube list plays; they then play at the speakers of each room (P plays in the room she's in, O switches between that room and everywhere; the brass panels in the studio and the record player do the same) instead of the YouTube list. It is copied into the build only for private builds: a public build (`PUBLIC_BUILD=1`, as on GitHub Pages and Netlify) leaves it out unless `PLAY_PERSONAL=1` is set on purpose.
- **Photo-scanned textures (optional):** on your own computer run `python3 tools/fetch_play_textures.py` from `gh18-house/` (~60 MB from Poly Haven, CC0), then commit `viewer/play/textures/`. Without them the page uses its generated textures.
- **Build:** a separate Vite config (`vite.play.config.js`), run after the tour's build by `npm run build`. The tour's own output is unchanged. Dev server: `npm run dev:play` (http://localhost:5174/play/).
- **Test:** `npm run build && node scripts/play_shots.mjs [outDir]` drives, walks and screenshots a fixed route headless.
- **Time of day:** a running clock with the real sun for the date (near Goa). T jumps between moments of the day; `[` / `]` slow down or speed up time, `\` stops it.
- **What you see and hear:** tyre smoke when the tyres slide on tarmac, sand and dust thrown up off the road, a splash in the shallows; the dashboard's gauges glow at night; tyre scrub, squeal and skid, gravel, splash, a gearbox whine and exhaust pops on a sharp lift.
- **Driving aids:** K (or the HUD button) cycles Assisted · Sport · Aids off. A gamepad works: RT / LT gas and brake, left stick steers, RB handbrake, Y get in / out, View camera.
- **Her reader and her paintings:** E (or a click) on a poem on the wall, or on one of the three e-readers in the studio (the atelier desk, the window bed, the book by the reading nook), and an e-reader comes up into her hands, the studio dimmed behind it (`viewer/src/play/ereader.js`). - **His letters (a treasure hunt):** twelve letters hidden round the world, each floating in a soft glow where it's waiting: the first on the passenger seat when she starts the drive, then the mandir, the kitchen, the master bedroom, the front terrace, the reading nook, the record player, the gallery bench, her desk, the court, the beach fire, and the last at the top of the lighthouse (it opens only once the others are found; then the sun goes down and sky lanterns rise from the beach). E or a click and the letter unfolds in her hands; ← → for more pages; a photo and his voice if given. The ✉ counter by the clock lists those found to read again; gold rings on the map show roughly where the rest are. - **Her library (her books):** PDFs in `viewer/play/deepti/books/` (gitignored: copied in by hand, never pushed), listed in `pack.json` → `"books": [{ "file": "books/name.pdf", "title": "…", "author": "…" }]`. ## house.json conventions

- **Units and origin.** Units are mm. The origin is the rear-left outer corner of the plot.
  - `x`: across the plot, left to right as the plan is drawn.
  - `y`: from the back lawn toward the street.
  - `z`: up from natural ground.
- **Levels.** `levels` holds the finished floor heights (GF +450, FF +3750, SF +7050).
- **Walls.** A wall is either a centreline `a → b` or an `arc` (`c`, `r`, `from`/`to` in degrees), with thickness `t`.
  - `kind`: external, internal, lift, shaft, boundary, parapet, glass_wall, glass_partition, glass_railing, railing, screen.
  - `status`: existing, new or proposed.
  - Optional `h` and `base` override the floor's clear height.
- **Openings** live inside their wall:
  - `offset` is measured along the wall from `a`.
  - `width`, `height` and `sill` follow the schedule.
  - `hinge` is `a` or `b`.
  - `swing` is the world direction the leaf opens into (`+x`, `-y`, …).
  - Move a wall and its doors and windows move with it.
- **Rooms.** Rooms are explicit polygons, carrying finishes and ceiling data. **If you move a wall, update the polygons of the rooms it bounds as well.** `check_dims.py` will flag anything that drifts from the PDF.
- **Furniture.** Each piece has a `pos` (centre), `size` `[w, d, h]`, `rot` in degrees and an optional `z`. The piece's local +y is its front:
  - `rot` 0 faces the street
  - `rot` 180 faces the lawn
  - `rot` 90 faces −x
  - `rot` 270 faces +x
